Conclusion for Paper 1:
- p99 TTFT (11-41s measured) >> what giant-doc irreducibility ALONE predicts (<1% of requests over-SLO
  at reasonable rates) => the tail is SEEDED by irreducible giant prefills and AMPLIFIED by prefill<->decode
  interference (12.6% decode stalls, 70% giant prefill chunks) + queueing under saturation.
- The tail is NEW-document prefill (uncacheable) + interference, NOT cache misses. => caching cannot move
  goodput@SLO here; the lever is prefill scheduling (SRPF-class, known/textbook) or the SLO/workload itself.
- Under saturation the effective per-request prefill rate is well below single-req peak (shared GPU with 256
  decodes), pushing more docs over the SLO => explains goodput@SLO=0 and its coin-flip sensitivity.
