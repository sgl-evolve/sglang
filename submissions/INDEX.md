## Body of work (5 papers) — one thesis: *goodput@SLO for long-document multiturn serving is a prefill-scheduling problem bounded by compute, not a KV-cache problem.*
On the fixed rate-sweep goodput@SLO protocol (Qwen3.5-122B hybrid, TP=8, 2-tier KV), I map and bound the **entire** lossless serving design space. The unifying result: goodput is set by how heterogeneous prefill work is scheduled plus a hard compute ceiling; **every KV-cache data-movement/retention axis is a bounded non-lever**, and the one lever (whole-request shortest-prefill scheduling) is textbook. The two *novel* mechanisms I designed are rigorous **negatives**, which is what makes the map airtight rather than untested.
- **P3 `goodput-is-scheduling` (capstone/map).** The complete design-space map + the head-of-line diagnosis (4 independent methods) + a parameter-free lever-selection procedure with a multi-corpus phase boundary. Whole-request SRPF lifts goodput 0/3→**4.0 req/s** (λ5), SLO-tail-bound below the measured raw ceiling ~4.7. Companions P1/P2/P4/P5 develop four axes in depth.
- **P2 `corpus-bound-goodput` (caching axis).** The document-reuse *mirage*: single-pass LRU = LFU = Belady = 0 avoidable re-prefill; caching cannot move goodput. GPU-confirmed live (3-policy eviction sweep: SLRU/LFU both degrade, recency>frequency for multiturn). Sharpest evidence goodput is decoupled from hit: the one knob that *does* raise hit (the `write_back` backup policy, +5–7 pp hit, +7% throughput ceiling, same-node) leaves goodput unmoved. Phase-boundary criterion (caching helps iff reuse stack-distance > cache horizon), validated on constructed + 4 real corpora.
- **P1 `cca-prefill-admission` (admission axis).** Prefill admission control is a *negative*: deferring cold prefills is self-defeating because the SLO tail **is** the cold prefills. Plus a TP-determinism systems lesson (per-rank wall-clock decisions deadlock NCCL).
- **P4 `rpb-chunking` (chunk-level scheduling axis).** My novel Reserved-Prefill-Budget mechanism is a *negative*: fixed reservation hurts the goodput rate via reserve waste (−13% prefill capacity, quantified), and the zero-waste adaptive variant is neutral → chunk-level *reservation* can't beat whole-request SRPF at any implementation. Negative scoped to reservation; a distinct primitive at the same granularity (queue-pressure-adaptive chunk *sizing*, which holds no budget in reserve) is left as future work.
- **P5 `kv-movement-nonlever` (transfer/movement axis).** The L2→L1 load-back is a *non-lever*: a large, load-growing volume (0.37→1.36 B tokens, λ3→λ10) moves at ≈10 M tok/s (HBM↔DRAM, paged direct IO), so every load-back is <30 ms — <0.2% of the queueing-dominated TTFT, on the critical path yet negligible. Bounds the whole movement-optimization family (prefetch, transfer/compute overlap, layout/paging, conversation co-residency). Slow-tier boundary: movement matters *iff* the hierarchy has a tier slow enough that its transfer is a material TTFT fraction — the movement lever lives *below* L2 (L3/disk), not within L1↔L2.

All same-node A/B (or engine-instrumented from the stock run), lossless-verified, adversarially self-reviewed. Chronological registry below.
